# Countermeasure grid (2026-11-17/18)

This directory holds every protocol of the common MDP framework, solved for
revenue and for disruption. The write-up is in
`studies/sop/docs/20261113_countermeasure_mdp_plan.md`, Steps 3 and 4; the
current findings are in `studies/sop/STATUS.md`.

## Files

- `grid.jsonl`: one record per solve (804 grid jobs, plus the SoP records
  listed below). Each record holds:
  - the job name;
  - protocol, alpha, gamma and objective;
  - the solver's value;
  - the reorg profile of the solved policy.

  Depth rates run to depth 40 and are rounded to 6 significant figures.
- `second_stage.jsonl`: `pcmdp.second_stage` on every revenue job. It gives
  the fewest and the most reorgs of 11+ blocks per block interval over the
  revenue-optimal policies, plus the profile of the grid's own (Howard)
  policy. All 264 records (completed 2026-11-18).
- `TABLES.md`: every table, generated by `tables.py`.

The MRL #146 SoP runs are in `../sop_hs_20261118/`.

## How it was run

The code is pow-countermeasure-mdp (GPL-3), `scripts/grid/` (tables.py at
ea07ded):
`gen_20261117.sh` writes the job lists, `run1.sh` runs one job, and
`tables.py` writes the files here, merging the grid outputs, the second stage
and `../sop_hs_20261118/`. All jobs ran on a 1 TB box with Howard (exact
average-reward policy iteration), 4 BLAS threads per job.

## SoP records in grid.jsonl

There are three SoP variants:

- `sop` (from `../sop_hs_20261118/`) follows MRL #146's rules: the attacker's
  own pending tip shares count (`--own-pending count`), and it uses the
  honest shares on the fork point (`--honest-shares`). These are the SoP
  numbers.
- `sop_ignore` (`soptight_*_dc`, caps x +-40, pending 24) uses our patched
  daemon's acceptance rule, without honest shares. It is a labelled variant.
  The caps bind: at alpha 0.40, x +-64 gives 6.676e-5 against 6.33e-5 here.
- `sop_count_superseded` (`soptight_*_dc_count`) is SUPERSEDED: its reveals
  counted the published pending shares twice (fixed in 08fb4b4).

## Units

- revenue (`_rev`): the attacker's share of main-chain blocks.
- disruption (`_dc`): majority reorgs of 11 or more blocks per block
  interval. A block interval is the mean time for the whole network to find a
  block (120 s on Monero). A majority reorg replaces the chain that most
  honest miners had.
- depth-weighted disruption (`_dd`): the summed depth of those reorgs per
  block interval.
- The reorg profile of a `_rev` job is per main-chain block. The second
  stage's rates are per block interval.

## Checks

- **Solver against profile.** Each job's solver value matches the stationary
  profile computed separately from its policy: revenue to 4e-11, and the
  deep-reorg rate to 8e-8 relative, over 264 jobs each.
- **Published values.** Revenue matches Zhang & Preneel at all 71 published
  points to 8e-5 (their 4-digit rounding):
  - one minus the "main chain block percentage" of `Results.xlsx`
    chainQuality, for Nakamoto at gamma 0, 0.5 and 1, UTB, UDTB, SHTB and
    PoP;
  - their Table V, for Subchains (ratio 2) at gamma 0, 0.5 and 1.

  RS, PRS and Fruitchains match Aumayr et al.'s `output.json` (port checks,
  8e-6 / 8e-6 / 2e-6).
- **Caps.** The cap checks (raising Subchains' cap from 20 to 25 and 30, and
  PoP's maxB from 20 to 25 and 30) move the 11+ rate by at most 4 % and 1 %.
  The values are in the plan doc, Step 3.
- **Second stage.** In every record, the revenues of the minimum, Howard and
  maximum policies agree to 1.5e-9.
