### Reading

1. **SoP v2 neutralizes both attackers, n = 2, with clean separation.** ES
   {0.035, 0.000} vs stock {0.377, 0.441}; lead-2 {0.000, 0.000} vs stock
   {0.305, 0.469}. Every one of the 2,822 subjective fork decisions on SoP
   nodes carried share weight; the attacker's blocks (no gossiped shares,
   arriving late) weigh 0 or one unit against ≈ 16 units per shared honest
   block and lose every race. Against the honest reference the attacker's
   canonical share is exactly 0 in all four SoP cells.
2. **The lead-2 blind spot is closed.** PoP-exact left the Qubic-style
   lead-2 policy at break-even (0.353 / 0.347 at mid-scale); SoP's share
   lateness (l_w) reaches proactive releases: the attacker's release carries
   no early-gossiped shares, so it is light regardless of when it arrives.
3. **The bridge is a biased reference under SoP.** In `es_sop2` draw 1 the
   bridge (stock fork choice) ended the run on a 5-block attacker branch the
   honest network had rejected (`share` 0.035 vs `share_h` 0.000, 5 differing
   heights); in draw 2 its tip trailed honest-001's by 11 blocks (439 vs 450).
   Report SoP numbers against the honest reference.
4. **Cost.** Throughput in SoP attack cells is 103–143 blocks per 6 h vs
   187–207 in the controls: the attacker's 40 % of hashrate is entirely
   discarded (expected ≈ 0.6 × 200 = 120), and the network orphan rate reads
   0.36–0.52 because every attacker block is an orphan. Vandalism is not
   removed, profit is.
5. **Non-upgraded honest miners lose races.** The honest control under SoP
   is {0.440, 0.326} (mean 0.383 ≈ α) but its second draw has 17 same-height
   races, all lost by the honest-strategy attacker whose daemon gossips no
   shares (orphan 0.218), versus 0 races in both stock controls and 1 in the
   first SoP draw. At n = 2 this is inconclusive, but it is the expected
   upgrade-transition property of the rule: a miner that does not share
   weighs one unit in every tie. A control with a SoP-flagged "honest
   attacker" would isolate it.
6. **Stock attackers earn less on the real-DAA base than on the genesis
   base.** ES {0.377, 0.441} (mean 0.409; ES γ=0 theory at α=0.4 is 0.484;
   measured γ 0.04 / 0.00) vs 0.463–0.492 on the retracted bases; lead-2
   {0.305, 0.469} vs 0.309–0.356. Spreads are wide (0.064 and 0.164); n = 2
   cannot say whether the base or luck moved them.
